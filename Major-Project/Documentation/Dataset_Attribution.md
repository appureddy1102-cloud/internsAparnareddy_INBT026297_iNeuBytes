# Dataset Attribution and Sharing Note

## Attribution

**Pharma sales data** by **Milan Zdravković**, Kaggle.

Dataset page: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/milanzdravkovic/pharma-sales-data

The Kaggle page describes six years of historical data (2014–2019), originally derived from pharmacy point-of-sale transactions, resampled to hourly, daily, weekly and monthly periods. It states that preprocessing included outlier detection/treatment and missing-data imputation.

## Safe repository practice

- Do not upload passwords, API keys, database credentials, patient information, or private mentor/student information.
- The supplied files contain aggregated category/date quantities, not individual patient records.
- Confirm that your mentor permits uploading the source and cleaned CSVs to a public repository.
- If source-file upload is not permitted, remove the CSVs and keep the Kaggle source link plus instructions for obtaining the data.
- Do not present the historical pharmacy data as your own primary data collection.
